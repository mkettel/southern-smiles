title: Drag to reorder stats in Wall view
tags: [feature]
visibility: admin
---

Stats can now be reordered vertically within each division column on the
Dashboard's **Wall view**. Useful for putting the metrics you check most often
at the top of each column.

## How to use it
- Open the Dashboard and switch to **Wall** view
- Hover over any stat card — a small grip handle appears on the left edge
- Drag the handle up or down to move the stat within its division
- The new order saves automatically and persists across sessions

## Notes
- Reordering is **admin-only** — employees see the same final order but can't drag
- The card itself still navigates to the stat detail page on click; only the grip handle initiates a drag
- Order is shared across the whole division, so stats from different posts in the same division will interleave however you arrange them
